---
title: "Optional Devices"
---

As mentioned before, mouse, keyboard and monitors aren't the important part for the GPU-Passthrough. Just pick ones you like for yourself.

<Callout type="success">
While not critical for the passthrough functionality itself, having dedicated peripherals for each system (host and VM) will provide the best user experience.
</Callout>

Choose the mouse, keyboard, and monitors that best suit your preferences.
